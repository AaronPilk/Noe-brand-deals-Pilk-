# Aaron's Negotiation Playbook — Noe Varner Brand Deals

Built from Aaron's actual sent replies, Jul 27 (evening) – Jul 28, 2026. Every quoted line below is verbatim from a real email. Use this to reply in his voice and hold his numbers.

Sources: TYPELESS (haimamedia), Pippit AI (AheadFour), Higgsfield (Jeevmedia), Viral Mind AI, Virlo, VidMuse (Superlinear), ScoutNow, TCE, Betatron, Internet People, Happy Oyster, Magic Studio, + the standardized five-slots pitch wave.

## 1. Voice & tone

**The register.** Two modes:

- **Pitch mode** (first touch): structured, positioning-heavy, signed "— Aaron / Talent Manager, Noe Varner". Opens "Hey {Name}," and always starts with the same frame:
  > "Appreciate you reaching out about {Brand}. Quick context on how we work, because it's different from most creators you'll talk to:"
- **Negotiation mode** (once numbers are live): drops to 2–5 sentences, opens "Hello {Name}," and signs off with just **"Thanks!"**. No pleasantries padding, no re-explaining. One move per email, one question per email.

**Signature tone markers (verbatim):**

- "Let's make this easy: let me know your maximum offer."
- "To make things easier please send you best offer and we can go from there."
- "That said, we don't do commission-only or CPM-only deals — full stop."
- "I will say that this fits Noe's niche well."
- "Since this is Higgsfield, a brand we really love, I am willing to go to $1250 per post but I would like to see 4 posts..."
- "Since this is our first time collaborating I am willing to accept the $1,200 price. One quick question: can we make it 3 videos at $3,000?"
- "One quick question, will you running ads on this video?"
- "What are you looking for?"
- "Is this something you would be interested in?"
- "If that is in your budget, we can discuss this further."

**Patterns to copy:**

1. **Concession always has a reason attached** — "since this is Higgsfield, a brand we really love", "since this is our first time collaborating", "if its just tiktok I will make an exception". He never just drops price; he names why, so the floor stays credible.
2. **Every reply ends in a question or a clear next step.** Never a dead end.
3. **"One quick question:" is his pivot phrase** — he uses it to attach a scope-expansion or diligence ask to an acceptance.
4. **Warmth without hype.** "Great to hear from you!", "Looking forward to meeting!" — friendly, never salesy, zero exclamation stacking.
5. **Declines stay warm and leave the number on the table** — "Thanks for the reply but we will pass for now."

## 2. Price architecture

**Public anchors (the five-slots pitch, sent to every new inbound):**

- **$10K/month partnership slot** — exactly five companies, one per category, category exclusivity, 3-month minimum, link-in-bio for the life of the contract, ongoing native content, ads run from our Meta account with monthly proof of spend. Details: noevarner.com/partners.
- **$2,500 single sponsored video floor** — "those start at $2,500 and we take very few."

**Real-world flex bands (what he actually closed/countered Jul 28):**

| Structure | Numbers | When |
|---|---|---|
| One-off video + platforms | "$2,500, with an additional $500 per social platform" | Standard one-off quote (TCE) |
| Deposit terms | "We require 50% deposit and remaining amount paid after video is agreed upon and posted." | Every one-off |
| Volume bundle | 3 videos at $3,000 ("a small discount for you") | Brand capped at ~$1,200/video (VidMuse) |
| Favorite-brand package | $1,250/post × 4 posts + link in bio 28 days + "possibly a 20% commission" | Brand he genuinely wants (Higgsfield) |
| Base + incentive conversion | "4 posts this month at $1,000 per post — $4,000 base." Incentives "kick in *after* the campaign value surpasses the $4,000 base" | Brand offers CPM/rev-share only (Viral Mind) |
| Niche-fit exception | $1,000 flat + 20% affiliate commission accepted | Perfect content fit, commission attached (Virlo) |
| TikTok-only exception | "We usually start at $2,500 but if its just tiktok I will make an exception." | TikTok-only campaigns (Pippit) |
| Hard pass line | $700/mo retainer → pass; "Our minimum starting price for video creation and posting is $2,500." | Anything under floor with no upside |

**The effective floor logic:** $2,500 is the *anchor*, not the true floor. He goes to $1,000–$1,250/post only when at least one of these is true: (a) genuine niche/brand fit, (b) commission/affiliate upside attached (20% is his number), (c) it converts into a multi-post package, (d) platform-limited scope (TikTok-only). He never goes below anchor AND single-post AND no upside at the same time.

**Rule of thumb: trade rate for volume, never just rate.** Per-post price can drop ~50% but total contract value must go UP (1×$2,500 → 4×$1,250 = $5,000; 1×$1,200 → 3×$3,000).

## 3. Objection handling by scenario

**"Your rate is above our budget" (no number given)** → Do NOT discount against yourself. Flip the anchor and make them bid:
> "To make things easier please send you best offer and we can go from there." (TYPELESS)
> "Let's make this easy: let me know your maximum offer. We usually start at $2,500 but if its just tiktok I will make an exception. Once I have a response we can proceed with the other details." (Pippit)

**Hard budget cap stated ($1,000–$1,200)** → Accept the per-unit price *if* fit + upside exist, then immediately widen scope:
> "Since this is our first time collaborating I am willing to accept the $1,200 price. One quick question: can we make it 3 videos at $3,000? That's a small discount for you and is more aligned with how we promote brands." (VidMuse)
> "I am willing to go to $1250 per post but I would like to see 4 posts, a link in bio for 28 days and possibly a 20% commission." (Higgsfield)

**Commission-only / CPM-only offer** → Refuse the structure flat, validate the brand, convert to base-plus-incentive:
> "That said, we don't do commission-only or CPM-only deals — full stop. Noe's content moves product for the brands he works with, and guaranteed base pay is how we protect the time and audience trust that makes that true." Then: "**4 posts this month at $1,000 per post — $4,000 base.** On top of that, your performance incentives kick in *after* the campaign value surpasses the $4,000 base." (Viral Mind)

**"Send your rates / media kit"** → Don't send a kit. Qualify first and point to the partners page:
> "To put a real proposal together I need three things from you. The budget you are working with, the deliverables you want, and your timeline. You can see Noe's audience and past campaigns here: noevarner.com/partners." (Virlo, Jul 21)

**Under-floor retainer / cheap UGC program** → Clean pass, leave the number as the door:
> "Thanks for the reply but we will pass for now. Our minimum starting price for video creation and posting is $2,500. If that is in your budget, we can discuss this further." (Internet People, $700/mo retainer)

**"No upfront payment, pay after posting" (agency policy)** → Hold the deposit line first: "We require 50% deposit and remaining amount paid after video is agreed upon and posted." (TCE — brand pushed back citing company policy; unresolved as of Jul 28. Default: hold 50% for unknown agencies; consider escrow/contract-first only with verifiable track record.)

**Exclusivity / usage / whitelisting asks** → Never bundled free. Link-in-bio duration is a priced lever (life-of-contract at $10K/mo; 28 days in the Higgsfield counter; 7-day asks get repriced). Ads run from *our* Meta account with proof of spend — that's the five-slots frame. Before accepting a low number, check who's boosting: "One quick question, will you running ads on this video?" (Virlo — paid usage changes the price.)

**Brand goes cold / campaign postponed** → Friendly close-out, keep the thread alive:
> "Thanks for the reply, let me know how the previous campaigns work out! Looking forward to hearing from you!" (Happy Oyster)

## 4. Deal-flow rules

- **Close in-thread** for one-off/video-package deals up to ~$5K. All of Jul 28's counters were closed by email, no calls.
- **Push to the call** (cal.com/noe-varner/partnership-call) the moment a brand wants to *explore structures* or is a slot-level ($10K/mo) candidate: "Yes, let's book a call. Here is our calendar link... Looking forward to meeting!" (Betatron).
- **Pass fast** on under-floor structures with no upside; always restate the floor so they can come back up.
- **Speed:** new inbound gets the standardized pitch within hours (batched); live negotiations get replies same-day, often within 1–6 hours. Brevity increases as the deal heats up.
- **Escalate scope, not friction.** Every acceptance carries a rider (more posts, commission, longer bio link). Every rejection carries an alternative.
- **Qualify budget before proposing.** "What are you looking for?" / budget-deliverables-timeline before any custom quote.
- **QA watch-out (real slip, Jul 28):** batched template replies went out with mismatched merge fields — the Tripo3D, CapCut, VidMuse/SSG and Luma replies all said "about SSG and the AI brands you represent", and the Luma thread reply greeted "Olivia" re: Wizstar. Proofread brand name + greeting on every batch send.
