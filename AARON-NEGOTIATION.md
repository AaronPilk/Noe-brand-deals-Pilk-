# Aaron's Negotiation Style — Noe Varner Brand Deals

How Aaron negotiates brand deals as Talent Manager for Noe Varner. Every quoted line is verbatim from his real sent mail (voice-raw.md, Jul 21–30 2026). This is the reasoning behind the numbers — for the drafting voice, see AARON-VOICE.md; for the full number bands and template skeletons, see NEGOTIATION-PLAYBOOK.md.

---

## Core operating rules

1. **Anchor high, then offer the cheaper tier.** Every first touch anchors two prices at once.
2. **Never bid against yourself.** When they won't name a number, make them.
3. **Trade every discount for more deliverables.** Price moves only when total contract value goes up.
4. **Concede with a named reason.** No naked discounts.
5. **Gate unnamed clients on a conflict check** before anything locks.
6. **Hold deposit and channel terms** as non-negotiable defaults.
7. **Say no warmly, restate the floor, leave the door open.**
8. **Concession order: window → volume → price last.**

---

## 1. Anchoring — the high-low frame

He leads with the **$10K/month slot** as the high anchor, then drops the **$2,500 single video** as the "cheaper" fallback. This is in every template.

> "Slots start at $10K/month. If you're looking for a single sponsored video instead, those start at $2,500 and we take very few."

- **States numbers as facts, not asks**, and line-items to justify a total:
  > "One Instagram Reel is $2,500, the 30 day ad code runs $2,000, and the 30 day link in bio adds $500, so $5,000 for the package as written."
- **"Start at" / "starting price" keeps the floor flexible upward**, never down:
  > "Slots start at $10K/month" · "those start at $2,500" · "Our minimum starting price… is $2500."
- **Positions audience quality before price** so the number lands against value:
  > "110K+ followers and 600+ paying community members — almost all business owners actively buying AI tools."

**$2,500 is the anchor, not the true floor.** He'll go to $1,000–$1,250/post only when at least one is true: (a) genuine brand/niche fit, (b) commission upside attached (20% is his number), (c) it becomes a multi-post package, (d) platform-limited scope (TikTok-only). He never goes below anchor AND single-post AND no-upside at once.

---

## 2. Never bid against yourself

When a brand fishes for a rate card or says "too expensive" without a counter, he **flips it and makes them commit a number first.**

> "Let's make this easy: let me know your maximum offer." (Pippit)
> "To make things easier please send you best offer and we can go from there." (TYPELESS)
> "What are you looking for?" (ScoutNow)

Only after they name a number does he shape scope: "Once I have a response we can proceed with the other details."

---

## 3. Trade rate for volume — the signature move

When he concedes on price, he **always asks for more units or better terms in return.** Per-post price can drop ~50% but total contract value must go UP.

> "can we make it 3 videos at $3,000? That's a small discount for you and is more aligned with how we promote brands." (VidMuse — 1×$1,200 → 3×$3,000)
> "I am willing to go to $1250 per post but I would like to see 4 posts, a link in bio for 28 days and possibly a 20% commission." (Higgsfield — 1×$2,500 anchor → 4×$1,250 = $5,000 + extras)

**Frames the discount as a favor to THEM:** "That's a small discount for you" · "both sides win big."

---

## 4. Concede only with a named reason

He never just drops the price — he names why, so the floor stays credible.

> "Since this is Higgsfield, a brand we really love, I am willing to go to $1250 per post…" (brand affinity)
> "Since this is our first time collaborating I am willing to accept the $1,200 price…" (first-collab)
> "We usually start at $2,500 but if its just tiktok I will make an exception." (limited scope)

*Watch-out: the named-reason discipline slips when he concedes fast on every pushback. Hold the rule — concession only with a named reason AND a scope trade, never in the first reply after pushback.*

---

## 5. Commission-only / CPM-only asks

Refuse the structure flat, validate the brand, convert to base-plus-incentive.

> "we don't do commission-only or CPM-only deals — full stop. Noe's content moves product for the brands he works with, and guaranteed base pay is how we protect the time and audience trust that makes that true."

Then the counter:
> "4 posts this month at $1,000 per post — $4,000 base. On top of that, your performance incentives kick in after the campaign value surpasses the $4,000 base." (Viral Mind)

Downside protection for Noe, uncapped upside for them. He accepts affiliate/commission **on top of** base (20% is his number — Virlo, Higgsfield), never instead of it.

---

## 6. The conflict-check gate (unnamed clients)

Before anything locks, he gates on client identity — an agency won't get a commitment without naming the brand.

> "Noe does not confirm campaigns for unnamed clients. He has several active conversations in the gen AI video space right now, so I need the brand name to run a conflict check before we commit, under NDA if your client prefers."

Paired with **channel discipline** — keeps everything on the record:
> "And let us keep everything on email so nothing gets lost." (Notably did NOT share WhatsApp when asked.)

And **authorization checks** on sketchy intermediaries: "Can you confirm you are the authorized contact for this brand?"

---

## 7. Deposit & payment terms he holds

> "We require 50% deposit and remaining amount paid after video is agreed upon and posted."
> "Our starting price for video creation and posting is $2,500, with an additional $500 per social platform."

- **Ads run from Noe's own Meta account with proof of spend** — a transparency lever, part of the five-slots frame: "ads run from our Meta account with monthly proof of spend."
- **Default: hold 50% deposit for unknown agencies.** Consider contract-first/escrow only with a verifiable track record.

---

## 8. Saying no — warm, floored, door open

Never burns the bridge. Restates the floor so they can come back up.

> "I am sorry we can not accept that. As you grow please feel free to contact us again!" (Meet August)
> "Thanks for the reply but we will pass for now. Our minimum starting price for video creation and posting is $2500. If that is in your budget, we can discuss this further." (Internet People)
> "No problem at all; thanks for being upfront. Do not hesitate to reach out to us anytime!" (TicNote)

On principle-based no's he's firm but explains WHY (see commission-only above).

---

## 9. Urgency & momentum — decisiveness, not fake scarcity

He doesn't manufacture urgency. Momentum comes from **next-step verbs** and reading buying signals.

> "get this rolling" · "get things moving" · "lock the content calendar" · "start filming" · "we'll set up a call"
- **Real scarcity is structural, not theatrical:** "exactly five companies, one per category" · "we take very few."
- **Reads persistence as a buying signal and names it:** "Appreciate the persistence on this one, it tells me you actually want it, so let me give you clean pricing…" (Magic Studio)

---

## 10. Concession order — window, volume, price last

When he gives ground, he gives it in this order:

1. **Window / duration first** — link-in-bio length is a cheap, priced lever he'll flex (28 days in the Higgsfield counter vs. life-of-contract at $10K/mo).
2. **Volume next** — more posts/videos to justify a lower per-unit rate.
3. **Price last, and only with a reason + a trade** — the per-post number moves only when total value rises.

Usage rights and exclusivity are **never bundled free** — they're separate line items ("those are two very different line items").

## Deal-flow rules

- **Close in-thread** for one-off/package deals up to ~$5K. **Push to the call** for slot-level ($10K/mo) or structure-exploration deals.
- **Pass fast** on under-floor structures with no upside — always restate the floor.
- **Escalate scope, not friction:** every acceptance carries a rider, every rejection carries an alternative.
- **Qualify budget before proposing** — budget / deliverables / timeline before any custom quote.
